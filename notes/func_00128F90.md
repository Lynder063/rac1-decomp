# func_00128F90 (00125630.c, _pictureCodingExtension, ee29)

Lombyte matches this with its own assembler. Ours is 508 bytes against retail 496.

1. Each IPU_CTRL (0x10002010) store is followed by a `func_00128A58` call. The compiler emits the store as a constant-address macro, `sw $6,268443664`, outside `.set noreorder`. Retail's assembler put `lui $1` before the jal and the `sw ...($1)` in its delay slot. Ours keeps `lui/sw; jal; nop`, which costs 3 nops.
2. Retail tail-calls the final `_nextBit(arg0, 8)` (`j`); ours returns normally, which costs 1 instruction.

Fixing (1) needs a pass like tools/check_macro_slots.py that moves the second half of a constant-address macro store into a following jal delay slot.
